Sky Gahshomar گاه‌شمار آسمان

The Zoroastrian calendars (Shahanshahi, Kadmi, Fasli, Bastani) from NASA JPL astronomy: roj and mah, the Gahs, festivals and the Moon.

Opening Sky Gahshomar ...
Open Sky Gahshomar
To put Sky Gahshomar on this iPhone's home screen: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, at the top right on iPad or the bottom on iPhone), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

If it has been asleep it shows "This app has gone to sleep": tap the button there and wait about a minute.

Put it on your home screen

The page must be opened in Safari on iPhone/iPad, or in Chrome on Android.

iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge): tap the three dots, then Add to home screen or Install app.

Then open it from the icon Sky Gahshomar (a gold Faravahar over a turquoise rosette band): it goes straight to the app.

The first time you tap Use my location in the app, allow location for the site. It is needed for sunrise, the Gahs and the Face-the-light compass.

Free, no ads, no account. Sun and Moon from NASA JPL (DE440s ephemeris). The Sasanian calendar is a reconstruction, shown for comparison. Your position is used only to calculate and is not stored.
